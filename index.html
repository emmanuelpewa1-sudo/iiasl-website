Local enterprise. Shared prosperity.

Empowering indigenous enterprise.
Building a stronger Sierra Leone.

IIASL unites entrepreneurs, partners, and supporters from Sierra Leone and the diaspora to promote sustainable trade, local development, and economic independence.

Through shared effort and generosity, our work empowers indigenous importers, uplifts youth and women, and builds a future of self-reliance and national pride. Together, we rise—strengthening local voices, championing fair trade, and shaping a prosperous Sierra Leone.

Who we are

Local voices.
Collective strength.

Indigenous Importers Association SL (IIASL) brings people and ideas together around a shared ambition: a more self-reliant and prosperous Sierra Leone.

By connecting entrepreneurs, partners and supporters at home and in the diaspora, the association champions local enterprise, sustainable trade and community progress.
