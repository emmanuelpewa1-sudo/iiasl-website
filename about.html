Who we are

Local enterprise.
Shared prosperity.

The Indigenous Importers Association SL (IIASL) brings entrepreneurs, partners and supporters together around a shared commitment to sustainable trade, local development and economic independence.

Our association

A collective voice for indigenous enterprise.

IIASL unites entrepreneurs, partners and supporters from Sierra Leone and the diaspora to promote sustainable trade, local development and economic independence.

Through shared effort and generosity, the association seeks to empower indigenous importers, uplift youth and women, and build a future of self-reliance and national pride.

Together, we rise—strengthening local voices, championing fair trade, and shaping a prosperous Sierra Leone.

Our priorities

What we work towards.
