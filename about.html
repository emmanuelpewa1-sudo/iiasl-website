The Indigenous Importers Association SL (IIASL) brings together entrepreneurs, partners and supporters who share a commitment to sustainable trade, local development and economic independence.

Our work is rooted in the belief that when indigenous businesses are connected and empowered, communities grow stronger and the country moves forward.
